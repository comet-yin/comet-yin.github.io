## 📜 The Class Tradition

As part of my Text as Data course at the University of Antwerp, I'm exploring how handwritten texts can be transformed into digital editions.

From scanning manuscripts to recognising handwriting and comparing different copies, this project is teaching me how technology can help us understand the history of texts.
